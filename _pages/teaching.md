Over eight years as a teaching assistant across three universities in Chile and Italy, I have taught mathematics in Spanish and Italian to audiences ranging from mathematics and physics majors to engineering, natural sciences, and future high school teachers. My approach pairs mathematical rigor with active problem-solving, relying on custom problem sets with targeted hints, collaborative activities, and interactive SageMath applets to build intuition alongside formal proofs.
