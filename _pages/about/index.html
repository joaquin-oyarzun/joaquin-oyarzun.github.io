I am a third-year PhD candidate in Computational Mathematics, Learning, and Data Science at the University of Pavia, working under the supervision of Marco Veneroni.

My research combines partial differential equations (PDEs) and the calculus of variations to study multiscale systems and their numerical approximation. I am currently extending this work toward continuum mechanics and scientific computing.

Before starting my doctorate, I obtained my Master’s degree at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile. Under the supervision of Duvan Henao and Manuel Sánchez, my thesis analyzed the variational structure and Backward-Euler time discretization of a monodomain FitzHugh–Nagumo system, alongside finite element simulations in Netgen/NGSolve.
