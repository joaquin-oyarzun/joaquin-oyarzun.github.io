I am a third-year PhD candidate in **Computational Mathematics, Learning, and Data Science** at the [University of Pavia](https://portale.unipv.it/en), working under the supervision of [Marco Veneroni](https://mate.unipv.it/veneroni/).

My research combines partial differential equations (PDEs) and the calculus of variations to study multiscale systems and their numerical approximation. I am now extending this work toward continuum mechanics, supported by scientific computing.

Before starting my doctorate, I obtained my Master's degree at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile, where my thesis (supervised by [Duvan Henao](https://sites.google.com/view/duvanhenao/personal-website) and [Manuel Sánchez](https://manuelsanchezuribe.github.io) analyzed the variational structure and Backward-Euler time discretization of a monodomain FitzHugh–Nagumo system, complemented by finite element simulations in Netgen/NGSolve.
